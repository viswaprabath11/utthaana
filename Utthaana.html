How to Become Better in Life

Three practical life learnings inspired by the teachings of Adi Shankaracharya.

1. Know Yourself

Adi Shankaracharya emphasized self-knowledge as a foundation for a meaningful life. Instead of being controlled by outside opinions and temporary circumstances, take time to understand your thoughts, values, strengths, and purpose. Through honest self-reflection and the habit of asking deeper questions about yourself, you can develop greater clarity and make wiser choices.

2. Discipline the Mind

A better life requires control over the mind rather than allowing every desire or distraction to control you. Practice concentration, moderation, and detachment from things that constantly disturb your peace. When the mind becomes calmer and more focused, you can study better, handle challenges with greater balance, and stay committed to what truly matters.

3. Grow Through Right Action

Personal growth is not only about gaining knowledge; it is also about living that knowledge through responsible and compassionate action. Treat others with respect, perform your duties sincerely, and reduce unnecessary attachment to praise or criticism. By combining knowledge, self-discipline, and compassion, you can gradually become a more balanced and purposeful person.
